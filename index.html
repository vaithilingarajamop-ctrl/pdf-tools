Install it

Same steps for both extensions.

  1. Download the .zip above and unzip it anywhere on your computer.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  3. Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  5. Open the extension and click Test Login to connect your Google account.
